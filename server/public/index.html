How to test side-by-side:

  1. Open chrome://extensions in your browser and click Reload on SyncWatch (or enable Developer mode and click Load unpacked to select syncwatch/extension).
  2. Open this page (http://localhost:8080) in two side-by-side windows.
  3. Click the floating 🎬 SyncWatch pill at the bottom-right of each window, type room name party, and click Join Room.
  4. Press Spacebar on Window A:
    • Window A's video pauses immediately.
    • Window B's video pauses instantly across the server.
    • Both floating widgets display the updated ⏸️ PAUSED status and sync toast.
  5. Press Spacebar again to resume playback across both windows!
